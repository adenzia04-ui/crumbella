Movement I · Anatomy

Every cookie is a movement of small parts.

Butter browned until it smells of toffee. Dough rested for a day and a half. Dark chocolate chopped by hand so it melts in pools, not dots. Each part has one job, and nothing is there for decoration.

Dough rest
36 h
Chocolate
70%
Weight
110 g

Movement II · The break

The real test is the snap.

A crisp, caramelised rim that gives way to a soft centre. When you pull it apart, the chocolate should stretch before it lets go. If a batch doesn’t do that, it doesn’t leave the kitchen.

Bake
11 min
Oven
180°C
Centre
Molten

Movement III · The brownie

Then, something darker.

Dense, fudgy and heavy for its size, with a paper-thin crackle on top that shatters under a fingertip. Made with more chocolate than flour, and baked a minute short on purpose.

70%

Movement IV · The cut

Nine squares to a tray.

Cooled overnight, then cut with a hot knife so every edge is clean. Each square is weighed, wrapped and sealed by hand before it leaves Cheras.

III · About

Ireen bakes in small batches, by hand, in Cheras.

Crumbella started in a home kitchen in Cheras with one cookie recipe and a lot of patience. Today Ireen bakes brownies and cookies for cafés, offices, weddings and anyone who thinks dessert deserves to be taken seriously.

Every batch is weighed to the gram, rested overnight and baked to order. Nothing waits on a shelf. When your box leaves the kitchen, it was in the oven that morning.

Who I bake for

  • Cafés & coffee barsWeekly wholesale trays, delivered across the Klang Valley.
  • Offices & corporate giftingBranded tins for Hari Raya, Chinese New Year, Deepavali and Christmas.
  • Weddings & eventsFavour boxes and dessert tables, from 50 guests up.
  • Birthdays & just becauseBoxes of 6, 12 or 24, baked the morning you collect.
